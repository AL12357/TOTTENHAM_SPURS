Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium is the home of Tottenham Hotspur Football Club. It opened in 2019 and replaced the historic White Hart Lane.

The stadium is also used for NFL games, concerts and other major sporting events.

Opened: 2019

Location: North London

Capacity: Approximately 62,850

Home Team: Tottenham Hotspur

Club Owners and Leadership

Biography

Daniel Levy has been one of the most important figures in the modern history of Tottenham Hotspur.

Role at Tottenham

As chairman, he has overseen major developments including the construction of Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Tottenham Hotspur Legends

Some of the greatest players to represent Tottenham throughout the club's history.

Jimmy Greaves

Striker | England

Biography

Jimmy Greaves is regarded as one of the greatest goalscorers in Tottenham Hotspur history.

Records
  • 379 Tottenham appearances
  • 266 Tottenham goals
  • One of the club's greatest scorers
Best Season

1962/63 — 37 league goals.

Career Story

Greaves became a key member of the Tottenham side that won major trophies during the 1960s.

Honours

FA Cup and European Cup Winners' Cup.

Danny Blanchflower

Midfielder | Northern Ireland

Biography

Danny Blanchflower was one of Tottenham's greatest captains and leaders.

Records
  • Legendary Tottenham captain
  • Leader of the 1960/61 Double team
  • Known for leadership and passing
Best Season

1960/61 — League Championship and FA Cup.

Career Story

Blanchflower became the inspirational leader of one of Tottenham's greatest teams.

Legacy

His famous words, "The Game Is About Glory," remain closely associated with Tottenham.

Glenn Hoddle

Midfielder | England

Biography

Glenn Hoddle was one of the most naturally gifted midfielders to play for Tottenham.

Records
  • One of Tottenham's greatest midfielders
  • Exceptional passing ability
  • Important player of the 1980s
Best Period

Hoddle played a major role during Tottenham's successful early 1980s period.

Career Story

He developed through Tottenham's youth system before becoming a first-team star.

Honours

FA Cup and UEFA Cup.

Steve Perryman

Midfielder / Defender

Biography

Steve Perryman is Tottenham's all-time appearance record holder.

Records
  • 854 Tottenham appearances
  • Club appearance record holder
  • Long-serving captain
Best Period

Perryman was an important part of the successful teams of the 1970s and 1980s.

Career Story

He spent most of his playing career at Tottenham and became a symbol of loyalty.

Honours

League Cup, FA Cup and UEFA Cup.

Ledley King

Defender | England

Biography

Ledley King was a one-club man who became one of Tottenham's most respected defenders.

Records
  • One-club Tottenham player
  • Club captain
  • Highly respected defender
Best Achievement

King captained Tottenham during the club's return to the Champions League era.

Career Story

Despite injury problems, King remained an important leader and symbol of Tottenham.

Honours

League Cup 2008.

Harry Kane

Striker | England

Biography

Harry Kane developed through Tottenham's academy and became one of the greatest goalscorers in the club's modern history.

Records
  • Tottenham's all-time leading goalscorer
  • Former Tottenham captain
  • Premier League Golden Boot winner
Best Season

Kane enjoyed several outstanding scoring seasons and won the Premier League Golden Boot multiple times.

Career Story

After loan spells away from Tottenham, Kane established himself as one of the world's elite forwards.

Legacy

He left Tottenham as the club's leading goalscorer.

Heung-Min Son

Forward | South Korea

Biography

Heung-Min Son became one of Tottenham's most popular modern players through his speed, finishing and commitment.

Records
  • One of Tottenham's leading modern scorers
  • Club captain
  • Premier League Golden Boot winner
Best Season

2021/22 — Premier League Golden Boot.

Career Story

Son became a key part of Tottenham's attack and formed a famous partnership with Harry Kane.

Legacy

He became one of the most influential Asian players in Premier League history.
